{#- Language on arrival. Every build remembers a choice made in the language selector (localStorage "hs.lang"). The English (root) build also sends a reader who arrives from outside the site to the same page in their language: the stored choice, else the first browser language the site has. It moves only to a page that exists in that language (its hreflang alternate). Crawlers and link previews stay on the address they asked for, so every language stays indexed. -#}
