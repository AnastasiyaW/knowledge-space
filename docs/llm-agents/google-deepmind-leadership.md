## What it is

A leadership and talent reorganisation, not a model or API release. On 2026-08-05
Demis Hassabis moved from Google DeepMind CEO to Chair and Alphabet Chief Scientist,
and Koray Kavukcuoglu became the SVP responsible for Gemini models, frontier
research, the Gemini app and the developer teams. Google also announced that Jeff
Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat were creating an independent public benefit corporation,
later identified as Discovery Loop. The announcement introduced no endpoint, model
id, pricing, availability or migration change.

## What changed

Google's 2023 structure named Demis Hassabis CEO of Google DeepMind and Jeff Dean
Google's Chief Scientist for Google Research and Google DeepMind; immediately before
this change Koray Kavukcuoglu was Google DeepMind CTO and Google's Chief AI
Architect. On 2026-08-05 Demis relinquished day-to-day operations for strategic,
scientific and advisory duties; Koray became the operational leader as SVP reporting
to Sundar Pichai; Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat launched a separate venture, whose
founders Discovery Loop later named as Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le and Oriol
Vinyals. Google named no replacement Google DeepMind CEO.
