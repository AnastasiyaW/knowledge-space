# About Happyin Knowledge Space

Happyin Knowledge Space is a free, open technical reference for software engineers and the AI assistants they work with. It covers more than two dozen domains, from Kafka, PostgreSQL and Kubernetes to diffusion models, LLM agents and security, with one focused page per topic: how a tool behaves, which settings matter, what changed between versions, and the mistakes that are easy to make.

## Who makes it

The knowledge base is maintained by **Anastasiia But** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/happyinhappy/)) — architecture, curation and the AI/ML pipeline — and **Anastasiya Ilukhina** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anastasiya-ilukhina-09646a289/)) — content and quality review. Anyone can propose a correction or a new article; see [How to contribute](contributing/index.md).

## How articles are written

- Each article is a condensed reference built from primary sources: official documentation, release notes, papers and measured results. Articles name versions and dates where behaviour depends on them.
- Drafting is assisted by AI tools; every article is checked by automated validation (structure, links, forbidden content) before it is published, and fast-moving domains are reviewed on a schedule (see the freshness policy on the [home page](index.md)).
- Readers and AI assistants can report an outdated or wrong page through the feedback link at the end of every article. Corrections are tracked in the public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AnastasiyaW/knowledge-space).

## Why it exists

AI assistants often state API flags, defaults and version-specific behaviour with confidence and get them wrong. A dense, source-checked reference gives both people and agents something reliable to check against. The same pages are written to be read by a person in a few minutes.

## Languages

The canonical articles are in English. Reviewed [Russian](https://happyin.space/ru/) and [Simplified Chinese](https://happyin.space/zh-CN/) branches are growing; a page appears in a language only after its translation has been reviewed.

## Funding and independence

The site is free to read, with no account or paywall. Its costs are covered by the maintainers and, where shown, by display ads from Google AdSense on article pages. Ads are labelled "Advertisement", never appear on the home page or on legal pages, and have no influence on what articles say. What is collected and how to opt out is described in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
