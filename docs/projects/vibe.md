# Vibe: vibe-max-subscription-tier — Vibe Max Subscription Tier

**Development line:** project:vibe · thread vibe-max-subscription-tier  
**Last event:** 2026-11-27 · 1 dated since 2026-11-27 · **Researched:** 2026-11-28 · confidence: medium

## What it is

Vibe is an AI copilot for dating conversations and date planning. It is for people on dating apps such as Tinder, Bumble and Hinge.

- Reply suggestions: written from uploaded screenshots, in the context of the chat.
- Conversation review: shows where a conversation drops off.
- Date ideas: based on location and budget.
- Goal guidance: steers the dialogue toward a goal the user sets, in the MAX tier.

Usage has a weekly quota. When it runs out, AI generation stops until the seven-day cycle resets. Vibe helps draft messages. It is not an agent that dates for you.

## Development line

- **2026-11-27 — VIBE reports telemetry on Cost-per-Date and monthly user date metrics.** The average Cost-per-Date for active VIBE users runs from $1.97 to $6.65. These users get between 2.58 and 8.34 dates per month.
  - The telemetry ties the $1.97 to $6.65 range to subscriber weekly usage limits and to the goal-based roadmaps in the Max tier. (2026-11-27) — <https://vibe.dating/>

## What changed

Vibe started with reply generation from screenshots. It then added structured conversation reviews and automated date planning. The Vibe Max tier followed, for custom conversational goals and personalized multi-step roadmaps. Cost-per-date telemetry and user conversion tracking came after that, to measure conversation outcomes against subscription spend.

## How to use this

1. Upload screenshots of dating app profiles or active conversations, so Vibe has the context.
  — <https://vibe.dating/>
2. Set your conversational goals and the tone you want in the Vibe Max settings.
  — <https://vibe.dating/>
3. Generate suggested replies, or read the conversation breakdown to find where the chat stalls.
  — <https://vibe.dating/>
4. Enter a budget and a location to get venues and itineraries for a date.
  — <https://vibe.dating/>
