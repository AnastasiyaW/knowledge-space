## What it is

HeyGen Avatar 6 is a video generation neural network that turns text and audio into automated presenter avatars. It competes directly with Synthesia and D-ID.

It can do this:

- gestures chosen from the context of the script
- motion prompts that script the choreography
- identity cloning from one photo or a short video
- synchronized lip-sync

It needs a clean 45-second reference video or a clear portrait photo. The free tier is capped at three one-minute clips per month, with a watermark. It is an alternative to physical studio recording for training, localized marketing and customer support presenters.

## What changed

Gestures used to be canned sequences on a loop. Now the model reads the script, and hand and head movements fire on keywords. Rendering moved to native high-definition resolution. Motion prompts let users script a specific gesture on cue.

## How to use this

1. Upload a high-resolution portrait photo or a 45-second reference video, plus a clean voice sample, to set up the avatar identity.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>
2. Enter the script in the editor, or upload a pre-recorded speech audio track.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>
3. Put motion prompts next to script words to set gestures such as pointing or nodding.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>
4. Before rendering, choose standard Avatar 6 for maximum fidelity or Avatar 6 Turbo for lower latency.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>

## Best practices

- Record audio clean and isolated, without background noise or room reverberation, so phonemes align and lip-sync stays accurate.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>
- Write the script naturally and add motion prompts only at key phrases. Prompting every sentence makes the gestures look unnatural.
  — <https://www.heygen.com/models/heygen-avatar-6>
