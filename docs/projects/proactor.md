# Proactor — Public launch and access

**Development line:** `project:proactor` · thread `public-launch-and-access`  
**Last event:** 2025-07-09 · 2 dated since 2025-06-18 · **Researched:** 2026-09-04 · confidence: medium

## What it is

Proactor is an alternative to Otter and Fireflies for meetings, calls, interviews, and classes.

- Live transcription during the call.
- Meeting Wiki for structured summaries.
- Action item extraction for follow-up tasks.
- In-call prompts to guide the conversation.
- Potor chat to query past meeting history.

Audio recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries sit on AWS servers in the United States. We can use it for meeting minutes and search across past discussions once participants agree to recording and access permissions.

## How to use this

From 2025-07-09, test Proactor through its website, waitlist, and onboarding routes instead of treating it as an unreleased project.

1. Create an account with Google, Apple, or email, then sign in to the app.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/creating-and-logging-into-you-proactor-account/>
2. Open Settings → Meeting Settings before the call and pick the transcription language spoken by participants.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/changing-meeting-settings/>
3. Select Start Recording so the service generates the transcript, Wiki summary, and action items.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/proactor-ai-features-overview/>
4. After the meeting, open Potor from the recording, home page, sidebar, or History → Chat to ask questions across past meetings.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/chat-with-potor/>

## Best practices

- Match the transcription language to the spoken language, as the documentation recommends for accuracy.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/setting-language-for-meetings/>
- Keep background noise down, prevent overlapping speech, and place the microphone close to the speaker for clean transcripts.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/tips-for-high-quality-audio/>
- Restrict sensitive recordings to Only me or share them with a password, because an open link gives access to anyone who holds it.
  — <https://help.proactor.ai/hc/article/control-who-can-view-the-shared-conversationprivacy%EF%BC%89/>
