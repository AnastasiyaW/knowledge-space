## What it is

Unitree Go2 is a commercial quadruped robot used by Haitian and Salvadoran forces for armed reconnaissance in Port-au-Prince.

Troops reportedly sent the robots ahead of soldiers into the stands and enclosed rooms of Silvio Cator stadium to search for ambushes. The quadrupeds carried IWI ARAD rifles mounted on improvised back brackets with magazines facing up.
