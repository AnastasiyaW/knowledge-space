## What it is

Origin F1 is AheadForm's desktop biomimetic humanoid robot for teams needing a physical shell with facial expressions and voice-visual interaction.

- Head, eye, and facial modules provide 26 degrees of freedom.
- Facial expressions, gaze, and blinking synchronize with speech.
- Vision and voice modules handle face recognition, visual and voice perception, speech recognition, and speech synthesis.

AheadForm does not publish a price, API, SDK, delivery terms, or independent quality metrics.
Origin F1 is a platform for HRI, not a verified ready-made product for autonomous tasks.
