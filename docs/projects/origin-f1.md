## What it is

Origin F1 is AheadForm's desktop biomimetic humanoid robot for teams needing a physical body with facial expressions and audio-visual interaction.

- 26 degrees of freedom move the head, eyes, and facial modules.
- Facial and vocal systems produce expressions, gaze, blinking, and speech-synchronized behavior.
- Vision and audio modules handle face recognition, visual perception, auditory perception, speech recognition, and speech synthesis.

AheadForm publishes no price, API, SDK, delivery terms, or independent quality metrics.
Origin F1 is an HRI platform, not a verified ready-made product for autonomous tasks.
