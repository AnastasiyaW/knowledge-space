# Kling AI

## What it is

Kling AI is Kuaishou's web and app platform for creators of short commercial, narrative, and character videos.
- VIDEO 3.0: text-to-video and image-to-video with native audio, multi-shot sequences, and dialogue.
- VIDEO 3.0 Omni: image, video, and element references for consistent characters or products.
- Motion Control and Lip Sync: motion transfer and voice dubbing for finished human characters.

VIDEO 3.0 generates 3–15 seconds per pass; Lip Sync requires a fully visible face and does not support animals.
Choose Kling for short controlled scenes with references and audio; assemble longer videos from verified shots.

## How to use this

As of 2025-06-30, evaluate Kling AI's video-to-lip and video-to-audio workflows separately when choosing a production route; detailed capabilities and availability still require source verification.

1. For a new scene, open VIDEO 3.0, select text-to-video or image-to-video, set a duration of 3–15 seconds, and enable Native Audio to generate sound with video.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide>
2. For a consistent character, product, or shot series, use VIDEO 3.0 Omni: create an Element from 2–4 images or a short video and apply it as a reference.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-omni-model-user-guide>
3. Describe the scene by location, subject, action, sound, style or emotion, and camera; put lines in quotes and assign them to a character.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
4. For finished footage of a person, choose Lip Sync, upload or generate audio, and check the result before publishing.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/ai-lip-sync-guide>
5. For specified motion, upload a movement video and character image, attach a facial Element if needed, and verify matching orientation.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide>

## Best practices

- Write prompts like a director's brief: scene, subject, movement, sound, and camera, rather than descriptive adjectives.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
- Use multiple angles or a short reference video for a consistent face; a single photo leaves hidden sides to model guesswork.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide>
- State clearly which character speaks each line to reduce voice confusion in dialogue.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide>
- Use only owned or authorized material for Voice Control; source audio must feature one speaker, 5–30 seconds, steady speech, and minimal background noise.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
- Use clips with a fully visible human face for Lip Sync, and fit audio length to clip duration.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/ai-lip-sync-guide>
