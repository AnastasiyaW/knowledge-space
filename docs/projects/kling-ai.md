# Kling AI

## What it is

Kling AI is Kuaishou's web and app platform for creating short commercial, narrative, and character videos.

- VIDEO 3.0 generates text-to-video and image-to-video with native audio, multi-shot sequences, and dialogue.
- VIDEO 3.0 Omni applies image, video, and element references to maintain consistent characters or products.
- Motion Control and Lip Sync transfer motion or add dialogue to finished human characters.

VIDEO 3.0 generates 3–15 seconds per pass; Lip Sync requires a fully visible face and excludes animals. Use Kling for short, controlled scenes with references and sound; assemble long videos from verified shots.

## How to use this

As of 2025-06-30, practitioners should evaluate Kling AI's video-to-lip and video-to-audio workflows separately when selecting a production path; their detailed capabilities and availability still require source-level verification.

1. For a new scene, open VIDEO 3.0, pick text-to-video or image-to-video, set duration to 3–15 seconds, and enable Native Audio when sound should generate with picture.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide>
2. For a recurring character, product, or shot sequence, use VIDEO 3.0 Omni: build an Element from 2–4 images or a short video and use it as a reference.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-omni-model-user-guide>
3. Describe the scene by setting, subject, action, sound, style or emotion, and camera; place dialogue in quotes and link it to the speaking character.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
4. For existing footage of a person, select Lip Sync, upload or generate speech, and check results before publishing.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/ai-lip-sync-guide>
5. For directed motion, upload a movement video and character image, attach a facial Element if needed, and verify orientation alignment.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide>

## Best practices

- Write prompts as a director's brief: scene, subject, motion, sound, and camera, not a pile of adjectives.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
- Use multiple angles or a short reference video for a consistent face; a single photo forces the model to guess unseen sides.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide>
- Name which character speaks each line to prevent mixed voices in dialogue.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-3-model-user-guide>
- Use only owned or licensed material for Voice Control; supply 5–30 seconds from a single speaker with even delivery and minimal background noise.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/klingai-video-26-audio-user-guide>
- Provide video with a fully visible human face for Lip Sync, and fit audio length to the clip duration.
  — <https://kling.ai/quickstart/ai-lip-sync-guide>
