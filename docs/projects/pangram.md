# Pangram: pangram-attack-dog-shaming-campaign — Pangram Attack Dog Shaming Campaign

Pangram is an AI text detector for publishers, universities and content platforms that checks long-form writing for machine-generated prose.

## What it is

Pangram looks for machine-generated prose in long manuscripts, essays and interviews. What it does:

- Scans a full manuscript for statistical traces of large-language-model generation.
- Gives a percentage probability for each text segment, interview and essay.
- Plugs into automated screening at manuscript intake.

Limit: it gives many false positives on stylized, regional or translated prose with uniform pacing or non-standard syntax. It works as a first triage flag for automated mass submissions. It should not decide a rejection without a human check.

## What changed

Pangram started as a statistical text-classification research tool. It is now commercial manuscript screening software used by international publishing groups. Model revisions put more scrutiny on long-form manuscripts, so it moved from academic essays to full literary prose. The more sensitive models turned out to be fragile on authentic regional dialects, oral histories and translated literary cadence. Publishers have responded by adding corroborating review steps.

## Best practices

- Never disqualify on a detector score alone. Check it against the author's drafts, interview notes and an editorial review.

- Allow for regional dialects, oral histories and non-standard narrative rhythm. They often trigger false positives in statistical classifiers.
