## What it is

Omma is a Spline product for designers and developers who build interactive web experiences without separate 3D, code, and media workflows.

- Parallel generation of code, images, and 3D
- File imports for CSV, JSON, DOC, GLB, GLTF, and media
- Remixes of community projects
- Publishing with custom domains

Free tier is capped at 50 credits per month and 20 chat messages. The product fits rapid interactive prototypes and published web projects, but generation requires credit planning.
