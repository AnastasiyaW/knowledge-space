## What it is

LMarena Video Arena is the video-generation route of the LMarena model-comparison site, where users compare AI video generators.

- Users: people comparing AI video generation models through the site.
- Key capability: a dedicated video modality, selected with the `chat-modality=video` URL parameter, with suggested starting prompts and a link to a leaderboard.
- Limits: the page does not describe how voting or the leaderboard is scored, and it names no separate video modes. The site's notice says conversations may be disclosed to AI providers and, in some cases, publicly.
