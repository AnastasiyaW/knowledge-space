## What it is

Unitree GD01 is a heavy electric robotic platform with an open seated cabin for an operator who needs a piloted civilian robot rather than an SDK-compatible research machine.

- Stance switching between bipedal and quadrupedal configurations.
- Control through a manual controller and partial autonomous walking.
- Secondary development unsupported on the retail model.

36 degrees of freedom, 32 kWh battery, 2800×1800×1200 mm, about 550 kg with battery and driver, 90 minutes of claimed runtime. We buy it as an operator system only after receiving current documentation from Unitree and written confirmation of the configuration.
