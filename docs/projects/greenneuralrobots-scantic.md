## What it is

Scantic is an on-device 3D scanner for iPhone on iOS 17 or later. We walk around a room, corner or single object while the app guides the capture and builds a 3D scene on the phone.

- 3D capture: guides filming to cover every angle without LiDAR.
- On-device reconstruction: turns footage into a 3D scene locally.
- Offline privacy: runs without an account or cloud upload.
- Web sharing: exports password-protected links and .spz files.

All processing stays on the device. Sebastian Beyer holds the 2026 copyright, and the site lists Vienna, Austria as its origin.

## What changed

The app is free on the App Store. The site outlines a three-step flow \(scan, process, explore\), on-device processing with a choice between a faster result and finest detail, and end-to-end encrypted link sharing with .spz downloads in the web viewer.

## How to use this

1. Install Scantic from the App Store \(free; iPhone with iOS 17 or later\).
  — <https://scantic.app/>
2. Start a new scan and walk in a slow circle around the room, corner or object while the app guides the capture.
  — <https://scantic.app/>
3. Process the capture on the phone, choosing between a faster result and finest detail.
  — <https://scantic.app/>
4. Explore the finished scan in the built-in viewer. Scans stay in the local library for re-processing or link sharing.
  — <https://scantic.app/>

## Best practices

- Scan in good light because more light improves quality. Slow down when the app prompts, and capture every side of the subject.
  — <https://scantic.app/>
- Use password-protected or private links for confidential captures. Scans encrypt on the phone before upload, and the decryption key remains in the link.
  — <https://scantic.app/>
