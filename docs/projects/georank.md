# GEORank: georank-product-development — Georank Product Development

GEORank monitors brand visibility across conversational AI models. It is built for marketing and search optimization teams.

**Development line:** project:georank · thread georank-product-development

## What it is

GEORank is an analytics and generative engine optimization platform. It is built for marketing and search optimization teams that track a brand's presence in large language models.

The service tracks mention frequency, competitor recommendations, brand reputation, citation sources, and optimization recommendations.

It covers ChatGPT, Alice, Yandex Neuro, Perplexity, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Claude. It works only through external query sampling and third-party citation tracking in the supported conversational search interfaces. It gives structured visibility diagnostics to organizations moving their organic search work to generative answer engines.

## Development line

- **2026-11-18 — GEORank adds AI-visibility diagnostics and recommendations.** The service now explains why AI assistants recommend competitors instead of a brand. It also offers recommendations for site and content changes, shows real user queries in a niche, adds an AI consultant, and tracks dynamics after changes. GEORank says over 300 companies use it and that it supports ChatGPT, Alice, Yandex Neuro, Perplexity, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Claude. This builds on an earlier July release, which only checked whether neural networks mentioned a brand.
  - The release adds citation source tracking, so it shows which external domains the models cite. It also adds sentiment analysis for brand mentions and one diagnostic report format across seven AI engines. (2026-11-18) — <https://georank.ru/>

## What changed

At first the service only checked whether a brand was mentioned in neural network answers. It has since grown into full generative engine optimization diagnostics:

- competitor recommendation analysis
- explanations of why a competitor is preferred
- discovery of niche queries
- recommendations for content and site structure
- an interactive AI consultant
- tracking of visibility metrics over time after the suggested changes are made

## How to use this

1. Submit the brand domains and core commercial product keywords to set up the tracking profile.

2. Select the AI platforms and query sets, so you get a baseline of mention frequency and share of voice.
  — <https://georank.ru/>
3. Read the competitor preference diagnostics and the cited external sources to find gaps in content and authority.
  — <https://georank.ru/>
4. Apply the suggested on-page content structures and third-party citation placements to raise the AI recommendation rate.
  — <https://georank.ru/>

## Best practices

- Get brand mentions into authoritative third-party media and industry directories, because generative engines often cite them as primary sources.
  — <https://georank.ru/>
- Track brand visibility and sentiment across several independent AI engines, not just one conversational model.
  — <https://georank.ru/>
