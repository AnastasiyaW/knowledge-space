## What it is

Doodles Stoodio is the user portal for the Doodles ecosystem. It connects avatar customization, wearable browsing, item collecting, and community activities.

- Avatar: creates and customizes Doodles characters.
- Packs: manages digital item packs.
- Wearables catalog: browses wearable items.
- Collectibles: gathers digital and physical items.
