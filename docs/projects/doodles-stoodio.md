## What it is

Doodles Stoodio is the user portal for the Doodles ecosystem. It brings avatar customization, digital packs, and physical collectibles together in one place.

- Avatar builder: creates and customizes a character
- Packs: opens digital items
- Wearables catalog: browses clothes and accessories
- Collectibles: tracks digital and physical items
