## What it is

OpenAI and Anthropic: AI pacing and regulation outlines vendor governance positions for teams assessing frontier-model safety.

- Staged release: manages deployment risks before full model access.
- External evaluation: provides embedded external evaluators with employee-like access.
- Capability checkpoints: discussed in policy commentary alongside openness around training processes.
- Regulatory coordination: proposed by Anthropic as coordination among frontier developers in democratic countries.

Neither vendor has established a verified joint framework. Treat these proposals as vendor-policy evidence, not a compliance standard.

## How to use this

1. Assess a vendor’s stated safety approach against the controls your organization needs: independent evaluation, documented incident handling, capability testing, and deployment safeguards.
  — <https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier>
2. Use staged access and release decisions when misuse risk is material, instead of treating publication as an all-or-nothing choice.
  — <https://openai.com/index/better-language-models/>

## Best practices

- Separate vendor proposals from binding standards. Request independently verifiable evidence before relying on safety or pacing claims in procurement or governance.
  — <https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier>
- Match release scope to demonstrated misuse risk, and revisit the decision as evidence changes.
  — <https://openai.com/index/better-language-models/>
