## What it is

NeyroSummit 19.0 is an autumn online conference on applied AI for practitioners. It offers working case studies and tools rather than theory.

The event runs in Zoom on Thursday 10 September 2026 from 10:00 Moscow time into the evening. It features 12 practitioners.

Topics include:
- Running a blog with AI
- One-person media company built on a swarm of AI agents
- Building an app to 10,000 installs
- Landing page built by voice in ChatGPT
- McKinsey-style market analysis with free tools
- Interactive landing pages with Midjourney plus Claude
- Automated AI video clips
- Team of AI agents using Hermes Agent
- Directing AI video

A free invitation is offered through a link to the Neyrohub bot's launch page.
