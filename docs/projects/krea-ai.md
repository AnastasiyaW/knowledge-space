# Krea AI

## What it is

Krea AI is a browser workspace for image, video and 3D work across generation, targeted editing, enhancement, animation and LoRA training.

Abilities:
- Generation: creates images from prompts and reference images across Krea and third-party models.
- Editing and animation: modifies regions, animates stills and upscales outputs.
- Training: builds reusable styles, characters or products with LoRA.
- Krea 2 controls: manages in-house style-reference, moodboards and creative direction.

Free access provides 100 compute units per day, with limited video, 3D, upscaling and LoRA access; paid generations include commercial licensing. We use it as a multi-model iteration surface and Krea 2 style-control route, not as a single fixed model or an unlimited free tool.

## How to use this

As of 2025-04-30, we assess Krea AI as separate video, training, editing, and enhancement workflows, selecting the named route for the task rather than relying on the homepage alone.

1. Open Image, write a prompt with subject, composition, style and lighting or upload reference images; choose a model and aspect ratio, then compare variations.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>
2. For Krea 2, use style references or a moodboard to guide visual direction and control how strongly references affect the result.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-api-launch>
3. Use Krea 2 Turbo to explore prompts, references and moodboards quickly; move the selected direction to Medium or Large when exact style adherence or final polish matters.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-turbo>
4. For a reusable style, character or product, open Train, create a LoRA from at least three related images, review captions, then select the finished LoRA in Image.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
5. Open Edit from a generated image or upload one, select only the region to change with a rectangle, brush or object selection, describe the edit, and iterate from the history strip.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-edit>
6. For final delivery, upload the chosen image to Enhancer, run Enhance, inspect the result, and download the output.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/enhancer>
7. To animate a still, open a generated or uploaded image in Video and set motion and camera direction with the selected image-to-video model.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>

## Best practices

- Keep a LoRA dataset focused. For a character, vary angle, expression, lighting and background; for a style, keep the visual language consistent without repeating one composition.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
- Review auto-captions and explicitly name details that must not be learned; start with one character, one style or one product identity rather than mixing concepts.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
- Use Turbo for rapid branching and Medium or Large for finals; Turbo preserves the controls but is not the recommended choice for maximum final-image fidelity.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-turbo>
- Keep the prompt fixed while changing one Generative Slider at a time. Use Raw or Low creativity for precise prompts, and Medium or High only when deliberate interpretation is wanted.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/generative-sliders>
- Edit the smallest necessary region and preserve the rest of the frame; use the edit history rather than regenerating the whole image after each revision.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-edit>
- For local Krea 2 work, train LoRAs on Raw and run them on Turbo; use Raw for fine-tuning or research rather than as the default final-inference checkpoint.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/krea-2-open-source>
- Compare outputs across models when readable text or strict instruction following matters, because text accuracy and adherence vary by model.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>
