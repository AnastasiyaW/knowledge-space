# Krea AI

## What it is

Krea AI is a browser workspace for image, video and 3D generation, targeted editing, enhancement, animation and LoRA training.
- Generation: runs prompts and reference images across Krea and third-party models.
- Editing and animation: alters regions, animates stills and upscales outputs.
- Custom models: trains reusable LoRA styles, characters or products.
- Creative control: runs Krea 2 with style references and moodboards.

Free accounts get 100 compute units per day, with limited video, 3D, upscaling and LoRA access. Paid generations include commercial licensing. Use it as a multi-model iteration surface and Krea 2 style-control route, not as a single fixed model or an unlimited free tool.

## How to use this

As of 2025-04-30, treat Krea AI as separate video, training, editing and enhancement tools. Pick the dedicated route for each job instead of relying on the homepage alone.

1. Open Image, write a prompt covering subject, composition, style and lighting, or upload reference images. Choose a model and aspect ratio, then compare variations.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>
2. For Krea 2, use style references or a moodboard to guide visual direction and balance how strongly references affect the result.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-api-launch>
3. Use Krea 2 Turbo to explore prompts, references and moodboards quickly. Switch to Medium or Large when exact style adherence or final polish matters.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-turbo>
4. For a reusable style, character or product, open Train and create a LoRA from at least three related images. Review captions, then pick the finished LoRA in Image.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
5. Open Edit from a generated image or upload one. Select the target region with a rectangle, brush or object selection, describe the edit, and iterate from the history strip.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-edit>
6. For final delivery, upload the chosen image to Enhancer, run Enhance, inspect the result, and download the output.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/enhancer>
7. To animate a still, open a generated or uploaded image in Video. Set motion and camera direction with the chosen image-to-video model.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>

## Best practices

- Keep a LoRA dataset focused. For a character, vary angle, expression, lighting and background. For a style, keep visual language consistent without repeating one composition.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
- Review auto-captions and explicitly name details the model should not learn. Start with one character, one style or one product identity instead of mixing concepts.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-lora-training>
- Use Turbo for rapid branching and Medium or Large for final outputs. Turbo keeps the controls, but higher tiers give better final fidelity.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-turbo>
- Keep the prompt fixed while adjusting one Generative Slider at a time. Use Raw or Low creativity for exact prompts, and Medium or High only when deliberate interpretation is needed.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/generative-sliders>
- Edit only the necessary region and leave the rest of the frame untouched. Step through edit history instead of regenerating the entire image each time.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-edit>
- For local Krea 2 work, train LoRAs on Raw and run them on Turbo. Treat Raw as a checkpoint for fine-tuning or research, not daily inference.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/krea-2-open-source>
- Compare outputs across models when readable text or strict prompt adherence matters, as model accuracy varies.
  — <https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-image-generator>
