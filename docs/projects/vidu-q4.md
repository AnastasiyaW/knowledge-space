## What it is

Vidu Q4 Preview is an early-access version of Vidu's next-generation flagship AI video model. Vidu says it focuses on expressive character performance, cinematic camera language and high-impact VFX. It accepts multiple image and audio references and outputs up to 4K.

## What changed

Vidu announced Q4 Preview as live on 7 October 2026. It starts at $0.014 per second. It supports up to 15 image references and up to 3 audio references, with 2K and 4K output at 10-bit color depth. Vidu offered a new-user invite code and a 72-hour promotion of 200 credits. On 10 October it issued a new invite code.

## How to use this

1. Sign up for Vidu with a new-user invite code \(VIDUQ4PV1 at launch; VIDUQ4PV3 announced on 10 October\) and try Vidu Q4 Preview. Pricing starts at $0.014 per second.
  — <https://x.com/ViduAI_official/status/2107856382263505219>
2. Supply references in one workflow: up to 15 image references and up to 3 audio references for characters, voices, worlds, camera language and VFX. Choose 2K or 4K output at 10-bit color depth.
  — <https://x.com/ViduAI_official/status/2107856382263505219>
3. For programmatic access, Vidu's profile points to its API at platform.vidu.com.
  — <https://x.com/ViduAI_official/status/2107856382263505219>
