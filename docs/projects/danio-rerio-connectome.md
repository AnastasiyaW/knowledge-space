## What it is

Danio Rerio Connectome maps zebrafish neurons and synapses as a vertebrate baseline for autonomous systems research.

A connectome maps the neurons and synapses of a brain. The fruit fly baseline has about 160,000 neurons, 10^7 synapses and about 100 TB of raw data. The zebrafish has a similar neuron count, about 10 times more synapses and about 200 TB of raw data. Unlike the fly, the zebrafish is a vertebrate connectome that shares basic structure with other vertebrates. A rodent is given as 70 million neurons and 10^11 synapses.

## What changed

Zebrafish mapping follows the fruit fly connectome with about twice the raw data, taking 200 TB versus 100 TB. The rodent is the next target after the fish. Connectome models are compact and resilient. Their design is the opposite of LLMs, but developers use LLMs to help build connectomes.
