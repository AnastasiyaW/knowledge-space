# Zhirinovsky AI

**Development line:** `project:zhirinovsky-ai` · thread `zhirinovsky-ai-development`  
**Last event:** 2023-06-15 · 1 dated since 2023-06-15 · **Researched:** 2026-09-05 · confidence: medium

## What it is

A political digital avatar built to answer in the style of Vladimir Zhirinovsky, not a verified public chatbot for daily work.

- Text and dialogue modes: answer prompts in character.
- Logic module: structure responses.
- Audience presentation: display live questions.

Trained on about 1 TB of video, text and images; 148 million parameters reported at the presentation.

We treat it as a historical political avatar because no live public interface or maintenance exists.

## Development line

- **2023-06-15 — Zhirinovsky AI was publicly presented at SPIEF.** LDPR leader Leonid Slutsky presented Zhirinovsky AI at the St. Petersburg International Economic Forum on 2023-06-15. The presentation showed a three-dimensional digital rendition. This was a public showcase, not a verified production release or operational deployment.

## What changed

- 2023-06-13 — LDPR announced that text, dialogue and logic modules were ready, scheduling audience questions for SPIEF.
- 2023-06-15 — SPIEF showcased the pilot as a digital apprentice rather than a clone, with no open public access planned.
