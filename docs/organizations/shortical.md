# Shortical: shortical-license-your-face-offer — Shortical License Your Face Offer

Shortical License Your Face is a portal where people submit their likeness for AI-made mobile micro-dramas.

## What it is

Shortical License Your Face collects facial likenesses for synthetic micro-dramas made for mobile viewing. It does three things:

- Applicant profiles: takes the intake form and personal social accounts for visual review.
- Casting screen: checks whether a face fits a character.
- Rights: acquires the likeness for generative avatar synthesis.

Pay and usage duration are not set at submission. They go into a second, separate contract. Commercial rates and residual rights stay undefined at that first step. The program cuts casting overhead for synthetic vertical video production.

## What changed

Shortical moved casting intake from physical auditions to digital profile evaluation. Performers now join an online registry to license their faces for synthetic mobile series. Money and duration are settled in a second agreement between the two sides.
