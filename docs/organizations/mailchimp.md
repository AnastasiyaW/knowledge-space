## What it is

Mailchimp is an email marketing and automation platform for small and mid-sized businesses, a competitor to Klaviyo and Brevo. It does three things:

- Email campaigns: automated sending and audience segmentation
- Transactional email and landing pages: delivery and publishing
- Customer journeys: cross-channel automation and CRM analytics

Limits: delivery depends on the deliverability policies of upstream ISPs, and pricing steps up with subscriber list size. Ownership is also about to change. It is still a standard choice for direct customer communication.

## What changed

Mailchimp went from an independent email tool to a subsidiary inside Intuit's financial products. Now it is set to be acquired by Bending Spoons. It would join a portfolio that includes Evernote and WeTransfer. Intuit moves its resources to internal AI financial tooling and keeps an equity stake in the buyer.
