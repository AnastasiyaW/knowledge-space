# Dodo Pizza: revenue-dashboard — Revenue Dashboard

## What it is

Dodo Pizza revenue dashboard is a public store-sales view for prospective partners and operators who compare Dodo Pizza locations.

- Country or store selection — choose a country or store.
- Store metrics — inspect revenue, orders, average ticket, year-over-year change and store age.
- Network comparison — compare one store with the network.

The Russian statistics page says it refreshes from the public Dodo IS API every 30 minutes and labels revenue as the previous month. Use it to investigate operating context and comparable locations, not as an audited company financial statement.

## What changed

The public dashboard provides store- and country-level sales data through filters and per-store comparison metrics. Available evidence does not establish that the reviewed date was its original release date.

## How to use this

1. Open the country page and choose a country or store. Then use the store table to inspect opening date and previous-month revenue.
  — <https://dodostats.ru/ru/pizzerias/rus/>
2. For a comparison, set the brand, country, currency and time-window filters. Review sales, year-over-year change, orders and unit age together.
  — <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dodobrands_most-franchise-systems-show-you-the-winners-activity-7464658865737957376-2nBe>

## Best practices

- Compare like-for-like locations. For a newer store, treat a missing or zero year-over-year value as the absence of a same-period baseline, not as evidence of no growth.
  — <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dodobrands_most-franchise-systems-show-you-the-winners-activity-7464658865737957376-2nBe>
- Before sharing a comparison, record the country, currency, period and filters used. The dashboard exposes different network slices and local currencies.
  — <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dodobrands_most-franchise-systems-show-you-the-winners-activity-7464658865737957376-2nBe>
