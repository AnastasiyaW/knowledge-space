## What it is

Decart is an AI efficiency engineering company. Its Decart Optimization Stack speeds up model training and inference across mixed AI accelerators.

What it does:

- Compilation and kernel optimization for AI chips.
- Workload migration between Nvidia GPUs and custom ASICs like AWS Trainium.
- Memory footprint reduction for real-time generative models.

Consumer generative models such as Lucy and Oasis stay separate from the core infrastructure stack that goes to cloud providers. Enterprise infrastructure teams get hardware portability and lower inference latency when they deploy large-scale neural network pipelines.

## What changed

Decart started with playable generative world models and real-time inference architectures. Its focus then widened to low-level hardware optimization through the Decart Optimization Stack. The aim was efficiency across accelerator clusters from several vendors. It ended with Amazon Annapurna Labs acquiring the optimization technology and engineering team, as the software base for Trainium silicon sales.
