## What it is

Spotta Bed Pod is an automated pest-monitoring IoT device with a notification platform. It is built for hotel operators and pest control contractors who deal with bed-bug infestations. It does the following:

- Lure: passively draws bed bugs into an enclosed chamber under the guest bed.
- Camera: an ultra-low-power camera takes an image when an insect enters.
- Classifier: machine learning tells bed bugs apart from other insects and debris.
- Alerts: sends a room-level alert with a photo to hotel management over the wireless link.
- Uptime: runs 24/7 on a long-life battery, with the lure replaced once a year.

It only detects pests that physically enter the pod. Once an alert comes in, a professional pest service still has to do the chemical or heat treatment. In practice it is an early-warning layer: room-specific alerts replace guest complaints as the first sign of trouble.

## What changed

Hotel pest control moved from manual visual inspection and reactive complaint handling to continuous automated monitoring. Early deployments went into low-power hardware that could sit under a mattress without frequent battery changes or disturbing guests. Later iterations tuned the vision model to spot bed bugs in varied lighting and orientations, which cut false positives from dust and other insects. Deployment then grew to large hotel chains through subscription packages with a central alert dashboard and links to commercial pest control providers.

## Best practices

- Put pods in every room, not a sample, so a spread to adjacent rooms is caught early.
  — <https://www.spotta.co/ar/post/find-bed-bugs-before-your-guests-do-24-7-monitoring-with-spotta-s-bed-pod>
- Send alerts straight to housekeeping and the on-call pest control contractor, so the room is contained before check-in.
  — <https://www.spotta.co/ar/post/find-bed-bugs-before-your-guests-do-24-7-monitoring-with-spotta-s-bed-pod>
