# Sber: ai-native-people — Ai Native People

Sber ai-native-people is Sber's research track on human-AI symbiosis, agentic systems and sociomorphic AI for engineering teams.

## What it is

Sber ai-native-people is a research and organizational track. It looks at human-AI symbiosis, agentic environments and sociomorphic AI, for engineering teams and enterprise operations.

What it covers:

- Linear-attention architecture modeling
- Autonomous multi-step agent orchestration
- Task-specific cybersecurity verification
- Sociomorphic behavioral simulation

It depends heavily on proprietary Sber infrastructure and on specialized Russian-language training corpora. It is a research framing, not a standalone commercial software package.

## What changed

The work started with traditional enterprise task automation. It moved to agentic systems that can run on their own for several hours.

The scope then grew to linear-attention mechanisms, so that million-token context windows are handled efficiently. After that came sociomorphic AI models, which simulate collective human decision-making and cooperative structures inside enterprise workflows.
