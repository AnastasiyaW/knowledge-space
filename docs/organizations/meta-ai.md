# Meta AI — Generative AI models

**Development line:** `organization:meta-ai` · thread `generative-ai-models`  
**Events:** 1 dated, 2023-07-17 → 2023-07-17 · **Researched:** 2026-09-04 · confidence: medium

## What it is

Meta AI is Meta's assistant for people using WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, the Meta AI app, and meta.ai.

- **Web answers** — answers questions, searches for web material, and summarizes it.
- **Plans and research** — makes plans and carries out research.
- **Slides and media** — creates them.
- **Email and calendar** — connects to them in some markets.
- **Recurring tasks** — performs them in some markets.

Agent features are rolling out only in selected countries and surfaces. This is Meta's product assistant, not a user-accessible version of the CM3leon research model.

## Development line

- **2023-07-17 — Meta AI announced CM3leon for generative text-and-image work.** A published research model works with both text-to-image and image-to-text. The paper reports FID 4,88 on zero-shot MS-COCO and five times less computation than comparable transformer approaches. This is research, not instructions for accessing today's Meta AI.

## What changed

Meta AI's line runs from a separate multimodal research project to a consumer assistant. Meta has not confirmed a direct product successor relationship between CM3leon and the assistant.

- 2023-07-17 — CM3leon: a research model was published for both text-to-image and image-to-text; the paper reports FID 4,88 on zero-shot MS-COCO and five times less computation than comparable transformer approaches. This is research, not instructions for accessing today's Meta AI.

- 2025-01-25 — The exact change is not confirmed: the event has no URL or title in the available material.

- 2025-01-27 — Meta began rolling out memory in personal chats and more personalized replies in the US and Canada; the memory can be deleted.

- 2025-03-19 — Meta AI began rolling out in 41 European countries and 21 overseas territories, initially as a chat in six languages across Meta's apps.

- 2025-04-29 — A separate Meta AI app appeared, built on Llama 4 and linked to meta.ai; voice, image generation, and image editing became available in the conversation.

- 2026-07 — Meta said it was rolling out features on Muse Spark 1.1: plans, email and calendar connections, slides, research, and recurring tasks through the Meta AI app and meta.ai, but only in selected markets.

## How to use this

As of 2023-07-17, practitioners tracking Meta AI's generative capabilities should include CM3leon as a text-and-image model milestone and consult its official material before drawing implementation conclusions.

1. Open the Meta AI app or meta.ai, and first check whether the needed function is available in your market.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
2. For a single task, describe the result and deadline: for example, a plan, research, slides, or a daily briefing.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
3. While Meta AI prepares a report, plan, or presentation, adjust the focus, tone, and unwanted sections directly in the conversation.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
4. In a supported group chat on WhatsApp, Messenger, or Instagram, call the assistant with “@MetaAI” and then write your request.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2025/03/europe-meet-your-newest-assistant-meta-ai/>
5. For a fully private conversation, choose Incognito chat if this mode is shown in your interface.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>

## Best practices

- Define an autonomous task as a result plus a schedule. This suits recurring briefings and reminders better than a general request without a deadline.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
- Do not wait silently for the final answer. Adjust the focus, tone, and length while the task runs.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
- Check the region and surface before starting a workflow. Meta explicitly says that new features roll out selectively.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-ai-muse-spark-doesnt-just-think-it-acts/amp/>
- For a group task, use the @MetaAI call, not personal memory. Meta described memory only for 1:1 chats, not groups.
  — <https://about.fb.com/news/2025/01/building-toward-a-smarter-more-personalized-assistant/>
