## What it is

Framestore is a production-services studio for teams commissioning screen and immersive work.

- Film and episodic: pre-production, virtual production, VFX, animation, supervision and creative technology.
- Advertising and immersive production: comparable service-led work.
- 3,000 artists and producers work globally; engagement starts through division-specific contacts.
- Commission it for a production workflow; the Superman 4D Gaussian Splatting work is a documented case, not a downloadable product.

## Best practices

- Bring the studio in before shooting when the brief spans planning, virtual production and finishing. Scope the cross-stage service mix rather than a finishing-only task.
  — <https://framestore.com/film-episodic-services>
- Use 4D Gaussian Splatting when a real performance must stay photographic while being reframed or disrupted in true 3D. Do not treat it as a universal substitute for character VFX.
  — <https://www.framestore.com/work/superman>
- Plan capture, scene alignment, splat rendering and compositing as one workstream. The reported Superman workflow used Houdini/GSOPs, a custom SIBR viewer, tracked cameras and 4K PNG output with alpha.
  — <https://radiancefields.com/gaussian-splatting-in-superman>
