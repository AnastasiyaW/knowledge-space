# Organizations

One page per company or lab the news conveyor follows.

## What an organization page holds

- Leadership and structure changes, dated, with the announcement that carries each one.
- Product lines and the events that moved them.
- What each change made obsolete - the older titles, structures and guidance.
- What remains unknown, stated as unknown.
- A short agent brief at the end for tools that read the page programmatically.

## How pages are made

Same discipline as the project pages: dated claims, first-party sources over undated biographies,
honest unknowns, and a freshness check that re-reads the sources on a schedule.

## Pages

Pages are listed here as they are merged. Leadership pages that predate this section live in their
topic domain, for example Google DeepMind under LLM and AI agents.
