## What it is

Semantica AI is a generative engine optimization platform for marketing teams that audit their presence in AI search tools. It runs alongside traditional search analytics.

What it does:

- Tracks brand citations and mention frequency in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini.
- Identifies the external domains that influence the generated answers.
- Analyzes competitor share of model voice.
- Provides reporting dashboards for AI search visibility.

The limit: coverage is queries sampled across the supported LLM interfaces, so results shift with model output. It is not built on standard web crawling indexes.

## What changed

Anton Kochenevsky, a search marketing practitioner, built Semantica AI to cover brand presence in conversational AI platforms. It started as bespoke prompt testing and became a software dashboard that measures brand citation rates across the major consumer language models. Early-stage venture funds focused on digital marketing and artificial intelligence then backed it.
